01

The short answer

All fourteen ideas together cost RM5,230 in cash and 356 hours — nine weeks of one person's full attention. That is not a plan, it is a wish list. Ranked by return per hour, cash efficiency and dependency, five are worth running now and nine have a trigger condition.

Finding 01

Paid ads are the last option here, not the first. Meta click-to-WhatsApp runs RM15+ per conversation and converts around 2.5%, so month one is cash-negative. Warm outbound returns RM45 a month for every RM1 spent.

Ads only make sense once the bot answers in under 30 seconds and there is footage worth running.

Finding 02

Time is the real cost, not money. Every channel in this list is affordable; the constraint is that the content engine eats 50 hours a month and Facebook groups eat 30 — and those two return the least per hour.

The bottleneck is your calendar, so hours are the column that decides.

Finding 03

Twelve of the fourteen end in the same WhatsApp conversation. So "automate marketing" is not fourteen projects — it is one agent behind every call to action, built once, serving eight of the plans.

That agent is also the product demo. The marketing and the demo become one artefact.

If you only do one thing

Run #02 Productised package then #01 Warm-network blitz — 42 hours and RM180. It produces the first paying client inside two weeks, and it produces the three things every other plan depends on: real chat footage, publishable numbers, and the first case study. The other twelve plans are amplifiers of that one motion.

03

Idea by idea

Each card carries the cash line items, the hours by task, the funnel arithmetic, and the split between what a machine can own and what only you can do.

13

Owner-audience creator pool

Paid amplification / third-party proof 70% automatable

Find small creators who are themselves shop owners. Give each a unique wa.me tracking link that opens the live bot, and pay on results — commission, no upfront fee.

Cash cost RM500

RM0 fixed. Pay on results: RM100–150 per signup or 20% for the first three months. Ten posts producing three clients ≈ RM500.

Time cost 20h

Recruiting 20 creators: 6h. Brief kit (script, assets, link): 4h. Day-to-day management: 10h.

ROI maths RM990/mo at day 90

  • FunnelApproach 20 → about 3 actually post → 10 posts/month → 60–150 clicks → 6–15 conversations → 2–4 signups.
  • Cash return2x per RM1 spent · payback Month 2 (pay only on results)

Can be automated

Tracking links, commission records, brief templates, a creator CRM.

Must stay human

Recruiting — it is a sales job — and screening who is genuinely a shop owner.

Run now · week 3 · The best-fit paid channel for this market: you borrow trust and only pay for results. Follower count is irrelevant; whether they actually run a shop is everything.

01

Warm-Network Blitz

Direct outreach 10% automatable

Message the merchants you already have a relationship with — current clients, suppliers, friends who own shops — using a short voice note, then a 1:1 call. In the demo you build an agent for their shop live, using their real menu or price list.

Cash cost RM30

No ads (RM0). Phone and data RM30. Demo number uses your own line (RM0).

Time cost 30h

List of 50: 2h. 50 voice notes at 8–10 min each including research: 8h. 15 calls: 5h. 10 demos: 10h. 5 proposals: 5h.

ROI maths RM1,350/mo at day 90

  • Funnel50 people approached → 12 calls → 6 demos → 3 clients (warm close rate 20–30%).
  • Cash return45x per RM1 spent · payback Immediate (first revenue in 7–10 days)

Can be automated

List tagging, follow-up reminders, auto-sending the proposal after a demo.

Must stay human

The voice notes, the calls, the live demo. Template them and the reply rate drops to zero.

Run now · week 1 · Highest cash efficiency and fastest money, but the ceiling is the number of merchants you know — exhausted in 2–3 weeks. Do it in week 1 and use it to fund the next six months.

02

Productised Vertical Package

Offer / packaging 85% automatable

Turn the service into something you can sell without a conversation: three tiers (Basic / Signature / Custom), a one-page site and a printed price card that states what is included, what is not, and how many days to go live.

Cash cost RM150

Domain + hosting RM150/year. Price card printing RM50. Design in-house (RM0).

Time cost 12h

Positioning and three tiers: 5h. One-page site: 4h. Price card and FAQ: 3h.

ROI maths RM450/mo at day 90

  • FunnelProduces no leads by itself. It is a conversion multiplier on every other channel.
  • Cash return3x per RM1 spent · payback Immediate — it lifts close rate on existing traffic

Can be automated

The page, the quote, the onboarding form, and auto-sending the price card plus booking link after each demo.

Must stay human

The pricing decision itself.

Run now · week 1 · Not a campaign — a prerequisite. 12 hours buys roughly +20% close rate across every channel (30 demos = 1–2 extra clients) and saves ~20 minutes of quoting per demo. Ship it before any outbound.

07

Partner / Reseller

Channel leverage 75% automatable

Web shops, POS resellers, e-invoice and accounting firms, small IT stores. Their clients already ask about WhatsApp; refer them and earn 20% recurring for 12 months while you do all delivery.

Cash cost RM250

One-pager printing RM100. Partner demo number RM0 (a bot number). First referral incentive RM150.

Time cost 29h

40-partner list: 5h. Partner kit (one-pager, script, demo number): 6h. 15 briefings at 15 minutes: 8h. Co-selling support: 10h.

ROI maths RM990/mo at day 90

  • Funnel15 briefings → 5 signed → 3 referred deals.
  • Cash return4x per RM1 spent · payback 0.25 months

Can be automated

The partner kit, the demo number (the bot demos itself), referral tracking, commission statements and reconciliation.

Must stay human

The briefing, the relationship, co-selling.

Run now · week 3 · The highest-leverage channel from month 2: clients grow, your hours do not. Five partners are worth more than 500 leads. Do not pitch partners before you have one case study — they are risking their own reputation.

06

Vertical SEO + AI-Search Visibility

Organic search / AEO 70% automatable

Three vertical landing pages in English and Chinese, five comparison pages (vs Wati, vs respond.io, vs the free Meta agent), and inclusion in roundup articles. The goal is to be cited by Google and by ChatGPT / Gemini when someone asks for recommendations.

Cash cost RM300

Hosting RM150. Bahasa Malaysia copy-editing outsourced RM150.

Time cost 38h

3 pages × 2 languages × 3.5h: 21h. Five comparison and roundup pages: 12h. Schema and internal links: 5h. Monthly price re-check: 3h.

ROI maths RM900/mo at day 90

  • Funnel60–90 day lag. Month 3–4: 300–800 organic visits/month → 15–25 leads → 2–4 clients by month 4–6.
  • Cash return4.5x per RM1 spent · payback Month 3–4 (no cash at risk while waiting)

Can be automated

Page templates, AI first drafts, schema, internal links, scraping competitor price tables.

Must stay human

Bahasa and Chinese editing — machine translation destroys trust — and the FAQ content.

Run now · week 1 · Highest long-horizon ROI in the list, but zero revenue for 90 days. Start now because compounding needs time. Chinese pages first: competition there is close to nothing.

Owned Audience Engine

Retention / CRM / community 65% automatable

Move everyone from every channel into a pool you own: a WhatsApp broadcast list plus one Telegram group for shop owners. Every CTA in every other plan feeds this.

Cash cost RM300

WhatsApp broadcast fees at roughly RM0.10–0.15 per marketing message: 500 contacts × 4 sends = 2,000 messages ≈ RM250. Tooling RM50.

Time cost 20h

Broadcast copy 8h/month. Group moderation 12h/month.

ROI maths RM900/mo at day 90

  • FunnelRequires 200+ contacts to mean anything. 500 contacts → 30 replies → 8 demos → 2 clients/month.
  • Cash return3x per RM1 spent · payback Month 2–3

Can be automated

Segmentation, scheduling, opt-in and unsubscribe handling, first reply.

Must stay human

A real person must be in the group, or it dies.

Month 2 · A second-order effect: every lead from every channel should land here, but it only matters from month 2–3. Design 'add to broadcast list' into every CTA now so the pool exists when you need it.

03

Paid Click-to-WhatsApp (Meta)

Paid social 60% automatable

Instagram and Facebook ads that land directly in a WhatsApp conversation instead of a form. Creative is real chat screen-recordings, so the ad is the product demo.

Cash cost RM1,800

Cost per conversation RM8–25 after the October 2026 repricing; budgeted at RM15 × 100 conversations = RM1,500. Creative RM300. Total RM1,800/month, ongoing.

Time cost 29h

Account and CTA setup: 8h. Creative edits: 6h. Daily optimisation 0.5h × 30: 15h.

ROI maths RM1,125/mo at day 90

  • Funnel100 conversations → 30 qualified → 10 demos → 2–3 clients.
  • Cash return0.6x per RM1 spent · payback Negative in month 1; turns positive week 5–6

Can be automated

First reply, qualification and booking — this is the only reason RM15 per conversation can pay for itself.

Must stay human

Creative refresh, audience testing, offer angles.

Month 3 · Do not run it before the bot answers in under 30 seconds. Wait for 1–2 case studies and usable footage, then start at RM500–800 to test — not RM1,800.

Client-side Amplification

Owned / earned 85% automatable

A waterproof QR sticker on every table and counter your clients own, plus one post from the client's own page tagging you. Their customers meet your bot before they ever meet you.

Cash cost RM250

200 waterproof stickers plus design RM250.

Time cost 24h

Design 3h. One hour of setup per client × 20: 20h. Writing the client's post: 1h.

ROI maths RM450/mo at day 90

  • FunnelDirect new clients are few: 2–5/month ≈ RM450–900. The real return is retention — each client kept is worth RM5,400 a year.
  • Cash return1.8x per RM1 spent · payback Retention, not payback

Can be automated

Per-client QR generation, scan data into the monthly report, sticker ordering, post templates.

Must stay human

Asking the client to actually publish the post.

Month 3 · A retention tool, not an acquisition tool. Roll it out after your tenth client and treat it as churn insurance plus a steady supply of case-study material.

Inbound Content Engine

Organic content 40% automatable

Personal IP plus niche content plus an AI-teaching page: three posts a week, mostly in Chinese, built on real chat screenshots and real numbers rather than software reviews.

Cash cost RM150

Tools (CapCut etc.) RM100. Small boosts RM50.

Time cost 50h

2.5h per post (topic 0.5, record 0.5, edit 1, captions and cover 0.5) × 12 posts: 30h/month. Comment replies 1h/day: 20h/month.

ROI maths RM675/mo at day 90

  • FunnelMonth 1: almost zero. Month 3: ~4 demos → 1.5 clients. Month 6: 3 clients/month, old posts still producing.
  • Cash return4.5x per RM1 spent · payback Month 3 for revenue; compounding after month 6

Can be automated

Scheduling, repurposing one video into three platforms plus a text post, caption and cover templates.

Must stay human

Topic selection, on-camera presence, the opinion. This is the moat.

Month 2 · The only channel that becomes an asset, and it needs a six-month commitment. Post three times a week, not five — five breaks you. Start in month 2 and share topics with the SEO plan.

AI & Search Ads

Paid search / AI placements 55% automatable

Buy the searches that already carry intent: 'chatbot WhatsApp Malaysia', 'WhatsApp auto reply', 'harga chatbot'. AI Max also places the ad inside AI-generated summaries.

Cash cost RM400

CPC RM1.5–4, budgeted at RM2.5. RM400/month buys roughly 160 clicks.

Time cost 14h

Account, keywords, negatives, landing-page match: 10h. Weekly optimisation 1h: 4h.

ROI maths RM540/mo at day 90

  • Funnel160 clicks → 8 leads (5%) → 4 demos → 1–1.5 clients.
  • Cash return1.4x per RM1 spent · payback Under 1 month

Can be automated

Bidding and budget, the landing page, the bot reply.

Must stay human

Keyword expansion and negative-keyword judgement.

Test only · Only a RM300–500 test. Malaysian search volume for these terms is estimated at 500–1,500 queries a month — you will exhaust the words quickly, and that is a ceiling, not an optimisation problem. Verify with Keyword Planner first.

Offline Trust Route

Events / associations 15% automatable

Join SME associations, sit inside F&B supplier groups, sponsor small events — trust that arrives through a shared contact rather than an ad.

Cash cost RM800

Association membership RM300. Two events (booth, sponsorship, banner, name cards) RM500.

Time cost 28h

12h per event (prep 4, on-site 5, follow-up 3): 24h. Relationship upkeep 4h/month.

ROI maths RM900/mo at day 90

  • FunnelTwo events → 40 conversations → 8 warm → 4 demos → 2 clients.
  • Cash return1.1x per RM1 spent · payback 2–3 months

Can be automated

Follow-up lists, QR into the bot, business-card scanning into the CRM.

Must stay human

The conversation itself and the relationship upkeep.

Defer · Only worth it for one kind of room: an F&B supplier group where 30 shop owners sit together. Skip generic chamber events. Go once you have a case study, otherwise you are just handing out cards.

Live Demo Funnel

Webinar + retargeting 50% automatable

A live session that tears down a real (anonymised) bill and builds an agent on screen, then retargets the replay to everyone who registered but did not attend.

Cash cost RM300

Replay retargeting ads RM300.

Time cost 22h

Script 4h. Demo environment 4h. Running it 2h. Editing the replay 4h. Ads 3h. Follow-up 5h.

ROI maths RM675/mo at day 90

  • FunnelNeeds an existing 300+ download or broadcast list — a cold live gets 8 viewers. 40 registrations → 15 attend → 5 demos → 1.5 clients.
  • Cash return2.3x per RM1 spent · payback Per session, week 3–4

Can be automated

Registration → bot reminders (day before, one hour before), the replay page, retargeting.

Must stay human

The live session itself.

Defer · after 300 contacts · A mid-funnel tool, not a cold-start channel. Wait until the broadcast list passes 300 people, then run four a year — no more.

04

Automation: what a machine can take

Break all fourteen ideas into their component steps and the rule is clean: "who to talk to" and "what to say" cannot be automated. Replying, qualifying, recording, delivering and reporting can all be.

StepPlansAutomatableHow it works — or why it must not
Lead lists and data 01 03 04 07 08 10 13 90% Map and Instagram scraping, industry tagging, de-duplication, CRM entry. Build once, reuse on every channel.
First reply / qualify / book 03 04 05 06 09 10 11 13 95% The highest-value piece. Point every CTA at your own agent: it quotes price, qualifies industry and size, offers two slots. Removes 60–70% of reply time and every lead experiences the product.
Delivery and onboarding 01 07 80% FAQ pack into a template agent so a new client goes live in 48 hours on templates, not overtime. This is where founder hours actually die.
ROI reporting 01 14 95% Generate from bot logs: conversations handled, after-hours captures, message fees saved. The day-30 report should never be hand-made.
Content production 05 06 13 60% Batch screen recording, one video into three platforms, caption and cover templates, scheduling, AI first drafts. Topic, face and Chinese copy stay human.
Commission and referral tracking 07 13 95% Unique tracking links, source tagging, monthly statements. Skip this and your partners stop referring by month two.
Outbound and DMs 01 07 10 10% Do not automate. Templated blasts are spam: reply rates collapse and the WhatsApp number gets restricted. Automate only the reminder of who to follow up with.
Relationships and trust 01 08 11 0% Voice notes, in-person demos, association events, live sessions. This is the only moat this market has — and it is exactly where the hours belong.

Build these five once — they serve eight of the fourteen plans

  • One agent behind every CTA, with wa.me/<number>?text= carrying a source code per channel (Instagram, TikTok, SEO, partner, creator). Solves auto-reply and attribution in the same build.
  • Content pipeline: script → bilingual captions → schedule → one master file cut for three platforms.
  • Lead-list builder: Maps and Instagram scrape → industry tag → de-duplicate → CRM.
  • Report generator: bot logs → day-30 one-page ROI report. This is a retention tool, not a formality.
  • Partner and creator reconciliation: tracking link → closed deal → monthly commission statement. Skip it and referrals stop in month two.
05

The 90-day sequence

The goal is not "finish fourteen plans". It is: three paying clients by day 30, one case study with real numbers by day 60, and a channel that fills itself by day 90.

  • Week 1#02 Packaging (12h), then #01 Warm blitz (30h). A price card before outbound, otherwise every demo is a fresh negotiation. Target ten demos and two to three clients. Start #06's first Chinese pages in parallel, four hours a day — SEO has a 90-day lag, so it has to start now.
  • Week 2–3Close #01 and deliver. Hold scope with the packaged tiers and go live in 48 hours on the template agent. By the end of week three you need one publishable number: how many after-hours enquiries were caught.
  • Week 3–4#07 Partners (29h) and #13 creators (20h). Partners need the case study first, so the order cannot be flipped; creators are commission-only, so they carry no cash risk and can start in parallel.
  • Week 5–8Deliver the rest of the pilots and finish #06. Keep recording every real conversation now, but do not start publishing yet — footage first, content second. The five running plans are already at full load here, and adding a content calendar on top is how week 6 becomes the week the delivery slips.
  • Month 2#05 Content engine, three posts a week (30h/month). Twelve shorts cut from the weeks 1–5 conversations; the best performer becomes the ad creative. In parallel: #09 turns the contact list into a broadcast pool (needs 200+), #12 spends eight hours on directory listings.
  • Month 3#03 Meta ads from RM500–800, once the bot answers fast, footage exists and there is one case study with numbers. #14 puts QR stickers in the first ten clients' shops — retention insurance, not acquisition.
  • Triggers#04 only after Keyword Planner proves more than 800 searches a month, then RM300–500. #08 only when invited into an F&B supplier group. #10 twenty minutes a day at most, killed if 60 days produce no demo. #11 once the broadcast list passes 300 people, four sessions a year.
06

Assumptions and caveats

Change one number and the conclusion moves with it

  • Price RM600/month at 75% gross margin → RM450 contribution per client per month (RM5,400 a year). Pilot price RM300 → RM225.
  • Churn is excluded. Your own time is excluded from the ROI figures and reported separately as hours.
  • Reference rates used when comparing: VA RM18/h, designer RM50/h, video edit RM80 each, developer RM60/h.
  • Ad and message unit prices are 2026 Malaysian market estimates; Meta conversation pricing already includes the October 2026 repricing.
  • "Clients at day 90" is the realistic case, not the optimistic one. Every ROI figure is the monthly contribution as it stands on day 90, not cumulative revenue.

What is not verified

Every unit price and conversion rate here is an estimate, not a measurement. Before money moves, back-fill three numbers from your own bot logs — cost per conversation, demo show-up rate, close rate. With those three, this page stops being a reading list and becomes a forecast you can steer by.
